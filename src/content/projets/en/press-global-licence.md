problem: "Readers who want several titles have to stack subscriptions, and most stop at one. Meanwhile, AI-generated answers in search engines are cutting traffic to news sites. A global licence, meaning a single subscription whose revenue is redistributed to publishers, regularly comes up in the debate. But the objection is always the same: what some would gain, others would lose."
approach:
  - text: "Start from public data, the audiences certified by ACPM (France's audit bureau of circulations), so anyone can check."
  - text: "Strictly separate three levels of information: what is sourced, what is estimated, and what is only an assumption."
  - text: "Model the redistribution title by title, using two keys: each publisher's contribution, and audience."
  - text: "Analyze precedents abroad, both those that worked and those that failed."
  - text: "Publish an online simulator where anyone can change the assumptions and see the effect on each title."
